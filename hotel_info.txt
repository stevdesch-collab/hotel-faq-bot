The Harbor View Hotel

Check-in: 3:00 PM. Early check-in from 12:00 PM is free if a room is ready; ask at the front desk.
Check-out: 11:00 AM. Late check-out until 1:00 PM costs $30, subject to availability.

Parking: Self-parking in the garage under the hotel, $25 per night with in-and-out privileges.
Valet parking: $40 per night.
EV charging: 4 chargers on garage level P1, free for guests.

Breakfast: Served in the Lighthouse Cafe, 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM daily (until 11:00 AM on weekends).
Breakfast is included with "Bed & Breakfast" rates; otherwise it is $22 per adult and $11 per child.

Wi-Fi: Free throughout the hotel. Network "HarborView-Guest", no password needed.

Pool: Rooftop pool open 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, May through September. Towels provided.
Fitness center: Open 24 hours, 2nd floor, use your room key.

Pets: Dogs under 50 lbs are welcome, maximum 2 per room, $50 per stay cleaning fee.

Front desk: Open 24 hours. Dial 0 from your room phone.
Airport shuttle: Free, leaves every hour on the hour from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Book at the front desk at least 2 hours ahead.
