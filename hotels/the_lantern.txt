The Lantern Boutique Hotel

Check-in: 3:00 PM. Check-out: 12:00 PM (noon).
Luggage storage: Free before check-in and after check-out.

Parking: No on-site parking. The City Center Garage is 2 blocks away and costs $38 per day; guests get a 15% discount with their room key.
Public transit: The Main Street subway station is a 3-minute walk.

Breakfast: Not served at the hotel. Guests get 10% off at Ember Coffee in the lobby, open 6:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Rooftop bar: The Wick, open 4:00 PM to midnight. Ages 21 and up after 8:00 PM.

Wi-Fi: Free. Network "LanternGuest", password is your last name.

Gym: Small fitness room on the lower level, open 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
Pool: None.

Pets: Cats and dogs of any size are welcome, up to 1 per room, $75 per stay. Pet beds and bowls are available on request.

Front desk: Open 24 hours. Dial 0 from your room phone, or text (555) 014-2290.
