Summit Pines Mountain Lodge

Check-in: 4:00 PM. Check-out: 10:00 AM. Late check-out is not available on Fridays and Saturdays; on other days, ask the front desk.

Parking: Free outdoor parking for all guests. Snow chains or 4-wheel drive are strongly recommended from November through March.
EV charging: none on site. The nearest public charger is in town, 6 miles away.

Breakfast: Hearty mountain breakfast in the Timber Room, 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM, included with every stay.
Dinner: Timber Room, 5:30 PM to 9:00 PM. Reservations recommended on weekends.

Wi-Fi: Free in the main lodge and cabins. Network "SummitPines", password "pinecone2026". Signal is weaker in the outer cabins.

Hot tub: Outdoor hot tub open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, year-round.
Ski shuttle: Free shuttle to Eagle Ridge ski area every 30 minutes, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM, December through March.
Ski storage: Heated boot and ski room next to the lobby.

Pets: Not allowed, except service animals.

Fireplaces: Every cabin has a wood fireplace. Firewood is free at the woodshed behind the main lodge.

Front desk: Open 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. After hours, use the red phone in the lobby for the on-call manager.
